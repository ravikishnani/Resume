How I run them

Every support function reports through operations. My job is to build the systems, define the accountability and coach the people who execute, so delivery is reliable, cost-efficient and accurately reported.

I work to an operating budget and a defined reporting line for every function. Skilled managers own their departments, a trained pipeline sits beneath them, and the SOP and KPI framework measures all of it — so the numbers come to me rather than me chasing them.

And I build it for the decade ahead, not the quarter. Capacity, headcount and systems are sized against where the business is going, so the operation scales instead of being rebuilt every time it doubles.
